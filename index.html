How it runs

Self-service, around the clock, no attendant.

The lot has no staff on site — that's deliberate, and it's why membership costs what it does. Your credential is the front door, and everything that needs a person happens on a schedule.

What that means for you

You open the gate yourself and plug in yourself, any hour. Detailing is booked by appointment. Vending is on site; the food truck runs scheduled days. If something breaks, the gate intercom rings a founder's phone.

Phone or tag

Open the gate from the app, or with an RFID tag on the windshield for fleet vehicles with rotating drivers.

Backup PIN

A keypad code for a dead phone, a guest, or a vendor working a scheduled window.

Free exit, always

Leaving never requires a credential. Nobody gets stuck inside a gate at 4 a.m.

Every entry logged

Time, credential, and a camera frame — so a dispute has an answer.

Billing runs the gate

Access is tied to your subscription. Cancel any time from your account; no phone call, no chase.

Plans

One flat rate. No per-kWh meter.

Charging is members-only, so a stall and a charger are always there when you arrive. Rates below are estimates — see the note under the cards before you reserve.

Premium

Why these are estimates

Final rates depend on three things we haven't signed yet: the commercial electricity rate at our site, the lease on the parcel, and pricing from our detailing partner. We publish our best estimate now and announce final rates before anyone is charged a cent.

Also available: vehicle storage from ~$75/month for a gated outdoor stall — add charging or a wash before pickup. Detailing without a membership, $80–150. Fleet plans for 5+ vehicles, quoted per account.
