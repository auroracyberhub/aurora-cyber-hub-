How it runs

Self-service, around the clock, no attendant.

The lot has no staff on site. That's deliberate, and it's why membership costs what it does. Your credential is the front door, and anything that needs a person happens on a schedule.

What that means for you

You open the gate yourself and plug in yourself, any hour. Detailing is booked by appointment. Vending is on site; the food truck runs scheduled days. If something breaks, the gate intercom rings a founder's phone.

Phone or tag

Open the gate from the app, or with an RFID tag on the windshield for vehicles with rotating drivers.

Backup PIN

A keypad code for a dead phone, a guest, or a vendor working a scheduled window.

Free exit, always

Leaving never requires a credential. Nobody gets stuck inside a gate at 4 a.m.

Every entry logged

Time, credential, and a camera frame — so a dispute has an answer.

Billing runs the gate

Access is tied to your subscription. Cancel any time from your account; no phone call, no chase.

Fleet & depot

Depot ground in the DIA corridor — built to a requirement, not to a guess.

Autonomous ride-hailing is arriving in this metro, and Denver has already restricted overnight fleet charging inside city limits. Aurora's Airport District sits outside those restrictions: industrial-zoned, highway-connected, and clear of residential buffers. We are assembling that ground and we are open to building it to a partner's specification.

Why we haven't signed land yet

Because a depot built to a guess is worth less than one built to a spec. We are screening parcels and utility capacity now, and we would rather site against a real requirement — vehicle count, charging type, timeline — than pour concrete first and negotiate second. If you are evaluating this corridor, we can share our zoning and utility screening work.

Path one

Host the infrastructure

We own and operate the ground and the charging; the fleet operator buys capacity — staging stalls, charging, cleaning and turnaround — without carrying the real estate or the buildout.

Path two

Operate our own fleet

If depot capacity is handled in-house by the operator, we intend to acquire and run our own vehicles on the network when third-party purchasing opens — based at a lot we already own and already service.
