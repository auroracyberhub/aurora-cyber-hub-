Why Here

Every fleet in this corridor needs ground. We're securing it.

Autonomous fleets are arriving in the Denver metro — and Denver has already restricted overnight fleet charging inside city limits. Vehicles still need somewhere gated, powered, and industrial-zoned to park, charge, and turn around. That's Aurora's Airport District, and that's Aurora CyberHub: a secure membership lot serving drivers, travelers, and fleets today, built to serve the autonomous operators of tomorrow.

1
Scarce ground: industrial-zoned, powered parcels near DIA are limited — early site control is the moat
2
Revenue today: memberships, vehicle storage, charging, and detailing earn from day one — no robotaxis required
3
Positioned for tomorrow: Tesla Robotaxi is testing in Aurora and Waymo is launching Denver — fleets that will need depot ground
Service Area

The DIA corridor, mapped for fleet operations.

Site selection is focused on Aurora's Airport District, I-1 and I-2 zoned parcels — chosen for direct highway access to DIA and clearance from the 300-ft residential buffer.

How It Works

Your car: gated, charged, and ready.

STEP 01

Join

Pick a membership tier. Your RFID access opens the gate 24/7 — no membership, no entry. That's what keeps the lot private and your vehicle safe.

STEP 02

Park & Charge

Pull into your stall under cameras and lighting. Plug in — Level 2 charging is included in Park + Charge plans, ready by morning.

STEP 03

Refresh

Grab coffee and wifi in the driver lounge, hit the food truck, or schedule a detail while you rest between rides.

Why these are estimates

Final membership rates depend on three things we haven’t locked yet: the commercial electricity rate at our chosen site, the lease terms on the parcel, and pricing from our detailing and service partners. We publish our best estimate today and will announce final rates before anyone is charged. If a rate moves, we’ll tell you why.
